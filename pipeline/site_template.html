1. Bets we're testing: does betting on the model pay?

Every bet is placed by a fixed rule from the game-morning DraftKings pull, nothing hand-picked. Flat 1 unit each; a player who doesn't play is a push. Expected is how many the model said would hit. A rule needs 150+ bets, a profit, and a hit rate above break-even before it's labeled trustworthy. Same-game stacks and the TD-price tracking are graded on hit rate only. 2. Everything the model projects: are the numbers right?

Every number the site publishes, bet on or not, checked against results once games finish: chances, floors and ceilings, averages, and the model's lines against the market. The verdicts below are generated by fixed rules from those numbers, not written by hand. Small samples are noise: a label changes only when the sample is big enough.
